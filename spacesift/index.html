SpaceSift

Get your storage back. Keep the photos that matter.

Photo libraries get bloated fast. Duplicates pile up, burst shots stick around, old screenshots never get deleted, and videos quietly eat gigabytes. SpaceSift scans your library and surfaces what you can safely remove.

iPhone & iPad· One-time purchase· No subscription

Download on the App Store ↗
What it doesScan · Review · Delete

  • Five kinds of clutter, one scan

    Duplicates, near-identical shots, blurry images, old screenshots and the videos taking up the most space. Each category shows how much you would get back, sorted by size.

  • You decide what stays

    Nothing is deleted without your say-so. The best photo in each group is pre-selected, but you can override any suggestion. Compare side by side and keep whatever you want.

  • Two safety nets before anything goes

    Queued items can be undone individually or cleared as a batch. After you confirm, iOS still keeps everything in Recently Deleted for 30 days.

  • Nothing leaves your device

    Scanning and analysis run locally using Apple's on-device frameworks. No uploads, no accounts, no analytics, no third-party tracking.

Interrupted? Progress is saved automatically, so you can come back later and pick up where you stopped. The dashboard keeps a running total of the storage you have freed, broken down by category.
