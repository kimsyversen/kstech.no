Promptuary

Stop rewriting the same prompts.

Your good prompts end up buried in old chats, random docs and notes you will never find again. Promptuary keeps them in your menu bar, ready the moment you need them. On Mac, press a shortcut and paste anywhere. On iPhone and iPad, tap copy and paste into whatever you are using.

What it doesPrompts · Fragments · Chains

  • Find any prompt in seconds

    Search by name, browse by category, navigate with arrow keys. On Mac the launcher pops up wherever you are working, and your prompts are indexed in Spotlight so they are searchable system-wide.

  • Variables keep prompts flexible

    Put {{placeholders}} in a prompt for the parts that change. Using it gives you a quick form to fill in, and recent values are remembered so you do not retype them.

  • Fragments are reusable building blocks

    Tone instructions, role definitions, project context. Change a fragment once and every prompt that uses it picks up the update.

  • Chains run multiple steps

    String prompts into a sequence where each step can pull in the previous step's output. Run the whole chain, or pause between steps to adjust.

  • One workspace on every device

    Prompts, fragments and chains stay together on Mac, iPhone and iPad. iOS adds a Markdown editor with formatted preview and one-tap copy.

  • Mac usage analytics and themes

    See how often each prompt gets used and when you last reached for it, tracked locally. Pick a built-in theme or build your own with every color editable.

Your prompts stay yours. Everything stays on your device. Turn on iCloud sync and it goes through your own iCloud account. No accounts, no analytics, no servers on our end.
