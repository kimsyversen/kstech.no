What it doesHRV · RMSSD

  • A circle breathes with you

    A visual pacer expands and contracts with each breath. Voice cues guide every inhale, hold and exhale, even with the screen off. Add haptic feedback if you prefer to feel the rhythm, or combine both.

  • Find your resonance frequency

    Everyone has a breathing rate where heart rate variability peaks. A guided 10-minute assessment tests six speeds and finds the one that maximizes your HRV.

  • Real data, per session

    Every session is logged with duration, heart rate, RMSSD and coherence metrics. Track streaks, view HRV trends over weeks and months, and save sessions to Apple Health.

  • Build your own protocols

    Any combination of inhale, hold and exhale durations. Enable an adaptive rate that adjusts to your coherence, or keep it fixed. Everything is optional.

Who it is for. People tracking autonomic regulation, athletes working on recovery, people with autonomic dysfunction seeking structured practice, and anyone who wants real heart rate and RMSSD data. If you are looking for a meditation app or a casual relaxation timer, this is not it.
